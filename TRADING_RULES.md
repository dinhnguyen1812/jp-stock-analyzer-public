# 📄 SHORT-TERM STOCK TRADING MEMO

## ⏰ PRE-MARKET PREP (8:45 – 9:15)
- ✅ **News Stock + Impact -> Analyze starred:**
  - Check news, themes (Nikkei, Yahoo News) + Open yesterday’s watchlist (if any)
- ✅ **Next feature**
  - Prepare trading journal (date, capital, blank rows)

---

## 🟢 ENTRY WINDOW (9:15 – 9:40)

### 🔍 Scan Criteria
- ✅ **Scan:** 
  - Volume surge > 1.5× average
  - Inflow is strong (positive)
  - Clean chart setup:
    - W-shape, triangle, flag
    - Breaking resistance or VWAP
  - Price still under ¥300
  - Bonus: Recent news or theme-driven

### ✅ Select 1–2 stocks
- Confirm with MooMoo: Total volume, inflow/outflow
- Read GPT analysis: Is there solid foundation for investing (recent news, technical indicators)?
- Max ¥100,000 per stock
- ✅ **Next feature**
  - Note entry price & volume

### 🛑 Set Stop-Loss
- ~5% below entry OR **support - 1 if entry * 0.95 <= support - 1**
- ✅ **Next feature**
  - Record stop price in journal

---

## 🕒 DURING THE DAY (10:00 – 14:30)

### 🔄 Monitor Actively
- ✅ **Next feature**
  - Analyze entried stocks
  - Track entried stock prices -> Calculate stop loss
  - Price going up?
    - ✅ Move stop-loss up (5% trail)
    - ✅ If breakout strong, widen stop-loss to 7–10%
  - Price pulling back?
    - Hold if near support or VWAP
    - Exit if breaks support + volume weakens

### 🧠 Midday Checklist
- Are you still in control emotionally?
- Are setups still valid?
- No revenge trading, no overtrading

---

## 🔔 TAKE PROFIT LOGIC
- Price up > 10%? ✅ Consider:
  - Sell 30–50%
  - Keep rest with trailing stop or for Day 2 open (gap-up)
- No breakout or volume fading? ⚠️ Exit all
- Strong close + theme/volume? ➕ Hold small portion with plan
