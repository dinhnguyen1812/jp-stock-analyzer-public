# 📄 SHORT-TERM STOCK TRADING MEMO  
*For stocks under ¥300 with volume surge*

---

## ⏰ PRE-MARKET PREP (8:45 – 9:15)
- ✅ Check news, themes (Nikkei, Yahoo News)
- ✅ Open yesterday’s watchlist (if any)
- ✅ Get ready with stock screener:
  - Price < ¥300
  - Volume already > 1.5× avg
  - Inflow ≈ strong
- ✅ Prepare trading journal (date, capital, blank rows)

---

## 🟢 ENTRY WINDOW (9:15 – 9:40)

### 🔍 Scan Criteria
- Volume surge > 2× average
- Inflow is strong (positive)
- Clean chart setup:
  - W-shape, triangle, flag
  - Breaking resistance or VWAP
- Price still under ¥300
- Bonus: Recent news or theme-driven

### ✅ Select 1–2 stocks
- Max ¥100,000 per stock
- Note entry price & volume

### 🛑 Set Stop-Loss
- ~5% below entry OR below support
- Record stop price in journal

---

## 🕒 DURING THE DAY (10:00 – 14:30)

### 🔄 Monitor Actively
- Price going up?
  - ✅ Move stop-loss up (5% trail)
  - ✅ If breakout strong, widen stop-loss to 7–10%
- Price pulling back?
  - Hold if near support or VWAP
  - Exit if breaks support + volume weakens

### 🧠 Midday Checklist
- Are you still in control emotionally?
- Are setups still valid?
- No revenge trading, no overtrading

---

## 🔔 TAKE PROFIT LOGIC
- Price up > 10%? ✅ Consider:
  - Sell 30–50%
  - Keep rest with trailing stop
- No breakout or volume fading? ⚠️ Exit

---

## ⏳ MARKET CLOSE (14:45 – 15:00)

### 📘 Review
- Sell any intraday positions unless swing plan
- Record:
  - Final price
  - Gain/loss %
  - Trade quality: ✅ / ⚠️ / ❌
  - Emotion note (was I greedy? fearful?)

---

## 🧠 RULES TO REMEMBER (Post-it version)
- 💸 Only trade top 2 setups daily
- 🛑 Stop-loss = capital protection
- ⛔ Don’t chase late surges without structure
- 🧘 No emotion = better results
- 📊 Review daily — log, learn, improve
